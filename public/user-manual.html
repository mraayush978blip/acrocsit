01

Marking Daily Attendance (Theory & Lab)

Teachers can take attendance on mobile phones, tablets, or laptops within 20–30 seconds per class.

STEP 1

Select Class & Subject

From your Faculty Dashboard, tap on your allocated Class card (e.g. CSIT-1) and click on your Subject.

STEP 2

Select Date & Time Slot

Date defaults to today. Choose the Lecture Slot (Slot 1, Slot 2, Slot 3, etc.) according to your college timetable.

STEP 3

Select Scope (Theory vs Lab Batch)

For Theory classes, select ALL. For Lab sessions, select your respective batch (B1 or B2).

STEP 4

Quick Bulk Toggle & Manual Overrides

Tap "Mark All Present" (turns everyone Green) or "Mark All Absent". Then quickly tap only the few absentees to toggle them off.

STEP 5

Save & Cloud Sync

Tap "Save Attendance". A verification modal displays the total Present & Absent count. Click "Confirm & Save" to sync.

💡
Pro-Tip for Fast Attendance:

Click "Mark All Present", call out only roll numbers of absentees, toggle them off, and save. Average time: under 20 seconds!

02

How to Edit a Particular Day's Attendance

If you made a mistake or a student was later marked Present with permission, you can rectify past records anytime.

STEP 1

Go to "Mark Attendance" Tab

Open the class and subject where the attendance was originally taken.

STEP 2

Select the Target Past Date & Slot

Click the Date picker and choose the exact date you want to edit. Select the Slot number that was marked on that date.

STEP 3

Auto-Load Existing Attendance

The system will automatically detect and load each student's saved status for that lecture slot.

STEP 4

Toggle Changes & Save

Flip the toggle switch of the students you want to correct. Click "Save Attendance".

STEP 5

Confirm Overwrite Modal

The confirmation modal will notify you: "Overwriting existing attendance". Click "Confirm & Save". Existing records are updated with zero duplicates.

03

How to Delete a Particular Day's Attendance

Use this if attendance was marked on the wrong date, wrong slot, or marked on an unforeseen holiday.

STEP 1

Open "Preview Attendance" / "History" Tab

Inside your subject class view, click on the Preview Attendance tab.

STEP 2

Filter to the Exact Date

Use the date filter in the toolbar to select the specific date you want to delete (e.g. 2026-10-18).

STEP 3

Click the Red Trash Icon

A red Trash button appears in the date summary bar when a single date is selected.

STEP 4

Select Slot in Delete Modal

A modal opens displaying all slots marked on that date (e.g., Slot 1, Slot 2). Check the slot(s) you wish to delete.

STEP 5

Confirm Deletion

Click "Delete Selected Slots". The session is safely removed from the student roster and percentages update immediately.

🛡️
12-Hour Safety Recycle Bin:

Deleted attendance is not destroyed immediately. It moves to the Recycle Bin for 12 hours. If deleted by mistake, you can restore it anytime before the 12-hour window expires.

04

Shift / Extra / Proxy Lectures

When taking a lecture on behalf of another teacher or taking an extra slot:

OPTION A

Shift Lecture Modal

Tap "Shift Lecture" button. Select the original faculty, target slot, and reason. The lecture will be logged under your subject with full audit tracking.

OPTION B

Extra Attendance Tab

For remedial, revision, or non-scheduled lectures, use the Extra History / Extra Attendance tool to credit students without disrupting normal slot statistics.

05

Class Coordinator Live Monitor

Coordinators have 360-degree visibility over all faculties assigned to their branch.

STEP 1

Access "Coordinator" Section

Switch to the Class Coordinator view from the navigation menu.

STEP 2

Open "Faculty Monitor" Tab

Shows every subject allocated to the class for today (or any selected calendar date).

STEP 3

Real-time Status Badges

✓ Complete: Attendance has already been taken and synced.
⚠️ Pending: Faculty has not yet taken attendance for today.

06

Generating Reports & Defaulter List (Excel)

Coordinators can export clean, official spreadsheets formatted for notice boards and HOD meetings.

STEP 1

Open "Reports & Analytics" Tab

Located inside the Coordinator dashboard.

STEP 2

Select Date Range & Subject Scope

Choose "Till Today" or specify a Custom Date Range. Choose All Subjects, Theory Only, or Lab Only.

STEP 3

Filter Defaulter Students

Select "Filtered" and set condition (e.g. ≤ 75%). The table instantly isolates students who do not meet the mandatory attendance criteria.

STEP 4

Download Official Excel Sheet

Click "Download Excel Report". The generated .xlsx file includes S.No, Enrollment ID, Roll No, Student Name, Subject-wise attended/total counts, and Aggregate Attendance Percentage.
